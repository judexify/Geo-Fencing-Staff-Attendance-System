This is the project brief that shows the what, who and what

## What Problems Are We Solving?

Clubs and similar establishments need a reliable way to
monitor employee attendance and maintain accurate attendance records.

The management needs to know

- who came to work?
- who arrived late?
- who hasnt shown up
- who clocked out
- review attendance records

The employees should be able to clock in and clock out within the premises

## Who Experiences The Problem?

- Employees
- Management

## What Outcomes

The employees should be able to clock in and clock out within the premises and management should be able to review attendance and activities of employees
